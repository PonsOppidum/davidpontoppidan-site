About

Why do large organisations struggle with AI when the technology clearly works? That question has shaped my career, even if it hasn't only been about AI. The answer, it turns out, has less to do with algorithms and more to do with how institutions make decisions, distribute authority, and codify judgment – problems that behavioural economics and sociology have studied for decades.

I lead Business AI across the Nordics and Baltics at SAP, where I work with enterprise leadership teams on the messy reality of AI adoption. Before that, I worked with Cloud Advisory, co-founded Capgemini Invent Denmark and scaled it to 30+ people, and worked in management consulting, primarily with Accenture. I'm completing an Executive MBA in Finance at Copenhagen Business School with a focus on AI monetisation and AI ROI in my thesis. Most boardrooms are still stuck on the wrong question, asking for the NPV of their investment instead of what real options on the future they are buying. Most companies are still monetising the wrong parameter, focusing too little on the outcomes they can servitise. I'm working to change that.

I hold a Gold Medal from the University of Copenhagen and a fellowship from Yale University, where I was a Denmark-America Foundation Scholar. My earliest professional life was as a Pashto language officer in the military – learning to operate in high-stakes, low-information environments where coordination was always king. That instinct never left.
